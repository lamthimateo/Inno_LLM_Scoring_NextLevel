CATEGORY 4: 3D REASONING / ARCHITECTURE (10)

A1. Why do arches distribute load more efficiently than flat beams?
A. Use less material
B. Convert bending into compression
C. Reduce gravity
D. Eliminate shear
E. Increase tension
Correct answer: B

A2. What happens if the keystone is removed from an arch?
A. Load redistributes
B. Structure remains stable
C. Arch collapses
D. Compression increases
E. Span shortens
Correct answer: C

A3. Why are triangular trusses structurally stable?
A. Equal angles
B. Fixed side lengths
C. No internal forces
D. Inherent rigidity
E. Symmetry
Correct answer: D

A4. Which transformation preserves volume in 3D space?
A. Scaling
B. Reflection
C. Translation
D. Shear
E. Projection
Correct answer: C

A5. Why do domes require buttressing at the base?
A. Vertical compression
B. Horizontal thrust
C. Thermal expansion
D. Material fatigue
E. Wind shear
Correct answer: B

A6. What changes when a cube is rotated 45° about a diagonal?
A. Volume
B. Surface area
C. Orientation only
D. Edge length
E. Mass
Correct answer: C

A7. Why do cantilevers require counterweights?
A. Reduce vibration
B. Prevent buckling
C. Balance torque
D. Increase compression
E. Improve aesthetics
Correct answer: C

A8. Which view best reveals spatial depth?
A. Plan
B. Elevation
C. Section
D. Perspective
E. Orthographic
Correct answer: D

A9. Why does increasing span increase bending moment?
A. Force increases
B. Lever arm increases
C. Density changes
D. Material weakens
E. Area decreases
Correct answer: B

A10. What defines a stable vault?
A. Symmetry
B. Constant curvature
C. Compression-only force paths
D. Uniform thickness
E. Rectangular base
Correct answer: C

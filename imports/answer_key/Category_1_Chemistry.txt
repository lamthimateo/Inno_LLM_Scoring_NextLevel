CATEGORY 1: CHEMISTRY (10)

C1. Which statement best explains why increasing temperature generally increases reaction rate?
A. It increases the equilibrium constant
B. It lowers activation energy
C. It increases collision frequency and energy
D. It stabilizes transition states
E. It changes reaction stoichiometry
Correct answer: C

C2. Why does a catalyst not change the equilibrium position of a reaction?
A. It increases forward rate only
B. It lowers Gibbs free energy
C. It speeds up forward and reverse reactions equally
D. It consumes reactants
E. It alters enthalpy
Correct answer: C

C3. What primarily determines molecular polarity?
A. Molecular mass
B. Number of atoms
C. Bond polarity and molecular geometry
D. Presence of hydrogen
E. Phase of matter
Correct answer: C

C4. Why is ATP hydrolysis exergonic under physiological conditions?
A. ATP has weak phosphate bonds
B. Products are stabilized by resonance and hydration
C. ADP is unstable
D. Pi has lower entropy
E. ATP decomposes spontaneously
Correct answer: B

C5. Which interaction is strongest in aqueous biological systems?
A. Van der Waals
B. Dipole–dipole
C. Hydrogen bonding
D. Ionic interactions
E. π–π stacking
Correct answer: D

C6. What is the pH of a solution with [H+] = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ M?
A. 2
B. 5
C. 7
D. 9
E. 10
Correct answer: B

C7. Calculate ΔG using ΔH = −40 kJ/mol, ΔS = −100 J/mol·K, T = 300 K.
A. −10 kJ/mol
B. −40 kJ/mol
C. −70 kJ/mol
D. −100 kJ/mol
E. +10 kJ/mol
Correct answer: A

C8. How many moles of gas are present if P = 2 atm, V = 12 L, T = 300 K (R = 0.082)?
A. 0.5
B. 1.0
C. 1.5
D. 2.0
E. 3.0
Correct answer: B

C9. For the reaction A ⇌ B, if [A] = 0.2 M and [B] = 0.8 M, what is K?
A. 0.25
B. 0.5
C. 1
D. 2
E. 4
Correct answer: E

C10. A reaction requires 2 mol A to react with 3 mol B. Starting with 10 mol A and 12 mol B, how many moles of product are formed?
A. 4
B. 6
C. 8
D. 10
E. 12
Correct answer: A

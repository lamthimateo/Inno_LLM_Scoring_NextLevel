CATEGORY 2: FEELINGS / EMOTIONS FROM PICTURES (10)

E1. Person laughs abruptly after a long silence — what emotion is most likely?
A. Nervousness
B. Humor
C. Relief
D. Frustration
E. Cannot determine
Correct answer: A

E2. Someone taps their foot rapidly while smiling politely.
A. Joy
B. Impatience
C. Anxiety
D. Confidence
E. Neutral mood
Correct answer: C

E3. Eyes looking down, shoulders relaxed.
A. Sadness
B. Shyness
C. Peacefulness
D. Indifference
E. Fatigue
Correct answer: B

E4. Person raises eyebrows slightly while leaning forward.
A. Curiosity
B. Fear
C. Confusion
D. Excitement
E. Suspicion
Correct answer: A

E5. A person pauses mid-sentence, blinking quickly.
A. Surprise
B. Stress
C. Dishonesty
D. Calm reflection
E. Disinterest
Correct answer: B

E6. Someone adjusts their clothes repeatedly before entering a room.
A. Nervousness
B. Excitement
C. Boredom
D. Frustration
E. Habit
Correct answer: A

E7. Person smiles faintly without eye engagement, posture open.
A. Content politeness
B. Genuine joy
C. Sarcasm
D. Cannot determine
E. Amusement
Correct answer: A

E8. A person’s breathing becomes noticeably slow and deep.
A. Anger
B. Relaxation
C. Fear
D. Confusion
E. Fatigue
Correct answer: B

E9. Rapid blinking paired with a tight jaw.
A. Stress
B. Anger
C. Joy
D. Embarrassment
E. Concentration
Correct answer: A

E10. Hands fidgeting but voice steady.
A. Deception
B. Mixed anxiety
C. Confidence
D. Relaxation
E. Indifference
Correct answer: B
